Choose where your app will live

Three routes lead to the same installable app. Pick one, then continue to Part 5.

  • Fastest

    Publish from AI Studio

    Good fit when
    You want a live HTTPS address with no GitHub or host account.
    Limits to know
    Your first two apps only, one Cloud Run region, and not available with a Google Workspace account or after paid Google Cloud billing.
    Updating later
    Publish again from AI Studio.
  • Part 4

    Vercel

    Good fit when
    You want automatic redeploys on every GitHub push.
    Limits to know
    The free Hobby plan is for non-commercial use only.
    Updating later
    Automatic after each GitHub push.
  • Part 4B

    Replit

    Good fit when
    You may charge for your app, or want everything in one dashboard.
    Limits to know
    The key is baked into a static build, so restrict it in Google AI Studio.
    Updating later
    Pull the changes and click Deploy again.

Before you begin

  • A stable internet connection
  • Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
  • An Android phone (optional)
  • 45 to 60 minutes of focused time
  • Willingness to follow steps in order
  • Adult accounts only, no child accounts
Security rulesNever share API keys publicly. Save all credentials in a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, or your browser's built-in manager).
