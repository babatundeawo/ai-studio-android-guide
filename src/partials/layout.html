    Add this guide to your Home Screen

    On iPhone and iPad there is no install button, so you add it from the Share menu.

    1. Open this page in Safari and tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. The guide now opens from your Home Screen like an app.
