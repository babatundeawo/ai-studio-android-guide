    Install this guide

    Installing puts the guide on your home screen. It opens full screen and keeps working without a connection.

    iPhone and iPad (Safari)

    1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. The guide appears on your home screen.

    This only works in Safari. If the page is open inside another app, use the menu there to open it in Safari first.

    Android (Chrome)

    1. Tap the three dots menu at the top right of Chrome.
    2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
    3. Tap Install to confirm.
